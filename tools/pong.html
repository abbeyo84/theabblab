LAB PONG

Press Space or click Start — auto-serves after each point

Controls

Left Paddle

  • W Move up
  • S Move down

Right Paddle

  • ↑ Move up
  • ↓ Move down

Game

  • Space Start / Serve
  • P Pause
  • R Reset

First to 11 points wins. After each point, the next serve starts automatically in a few seconds — press Space to serve sooner. Classic paddle physics — angle changes based on where the ball hits the paddle.
