About

The Laboratory

The Abb Lab is a creative studio built on curiosity — a space where Abbey experiments at the intersection of technology, design, and storytelling.

Part laboratory, part atelier. Every project starts as a hypothesis: what if we could make something beautiful, useful, and a little bit unexpected? From digital tools and visual media to merch and side experiments, this is where ideas are tested, refined, and released into the world.
