About

The Laboratory

The Abb Lab is the commerce and creative laboratory of ABBEYO ENTERTAINMENT — where limited merchandise, digital releases, and experimental media are designed, tested, and distributed.

Part storefront, part archive. The lab runs rotating collections — from Bitcoin-themed apparel to immersive art worlds like Strange Realms — alongside a growing library of audio, video, documents, tools, pictures, and AI resources shared on our terms.
